Automated Cooker Kitchen Appliance

In this project, I created a high-fidelity physical concept prototype of an automated cooking household appliance, in collaboration with the University of Colorado and the Idea Forge design center. This product allows for machine stirring and heating operations, automating core processes within the kitchen environment. This technology builds off of the kitchen appliance suite by applying the versatility of a mixing machine to a broader range of cooking actions. 
I built this prototype with a working motor system and heating instrument with variable heating modes. This product showed that woodworking and COTS electronics can be used to create high fidelity proof-of-concept or test prototypes.
