Algorithmic Product Design - "Tealight" Project

In this project, I used the software OpenSCAD to algorithmically generate a lattice structure. My first design was the following shell-like lattice structure for holding a tea candle. This was an exploration into the limits of algorithmically generated and patterned lattice structural designs. The result of this project that viable algorithmic, customizable, and scalable product design is feasible for practical purposes.
